# Prospect

Go from an ICP in "$ARGUMENTS" to a shortlist in one pass. Use Rebel MCP tools. If they are missing, follow SETUP.md.

## Examples

- `/rebel:prospect HR directors at 50–200 person SaaS companies in Sweden`
- `/rebel:prospect heads of sales at manufacturers in the Nordics`
- `/rebel:prospect CTOs at fintech, 200–1000 employees`

## Steps

1. If the ICP is missing a buyer **and** a market (size, industry, or geography), ask one question. Otherwise search now.
2. Search people in Rebel. If the market is unnamed companies, search companies first, then people at the best fits.
3. Show a shortlist. Unlock emails or mobiles only if the user asks — Rebel quotes the cost on the tool.
4. Offer to save the keepers to a list. List-add can unlock email; wait for a yes.
