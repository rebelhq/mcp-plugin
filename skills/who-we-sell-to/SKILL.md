# Who we sell to

Find the real customer. Don’t record the one on the slide.

Start with who they already won. If that’s thin, ask once: who closed last quarter — title and company type.

Say it like a founder in a board meeting. One sentence they’d stand behind. If the pitch and the wins disagree, lead with the wins.

Then check they’re not hunting ghosts — a sense of how many people like that exist, not a market essay.

Read the sentence back. Yes = that’s who we hunt from now on.

Never “X converts better.” Only “your last wins looked like this.”

- `/rebel:who-we-sell-to I think we sell to CMOs but I’m not sure`
