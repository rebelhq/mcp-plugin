# Campaign list

A working list for one motion. It has a name, a reason, and ~25 people — not a market scan.

"$ARGUMENTS" is the week (dinner, ABM, outbound, webinar). If they didn't name the list, name it for them from the motion.

If you can't tell who the buyer is, ask once. Otherwise go. Use who they sell to when the workspace already knows.

Each person gets one line: why them, this week. Stop at ~25.

When they like it, save it under that name.

## Examples

- `/rebel:campaign-list Thursday dinner in Stockholm, 30 seats, CROs who'd actually come`
- `/rebel:campaign-list ABM: Nordic SaaS that hired a CMO this year`
- `/rebel:campaign-list Q4 outbound — heads of sales at Swedish manufacturers, 50–200`
